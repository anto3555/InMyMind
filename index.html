Welcome to In My Mind.

In My Mind is an initiative that uses art and animation to invite people to see the world from different perspectives, one character and one story at a time.

Get to know me ↓

About me

Hi, I’m Antonella.

I’m from Peru, and I’m in the second year of the IB Diploma Programme. I’m especially interested in neuroscience and in how people experience the world differently.

I was diagnosed as autistic almost three years ago. Art has helped me ask questions, share ideas, and connect with people. I hope In My Mind can open that door for others too.

Why I started

I wanted to use something I love, drawing and animation, to make conversations about autism more thoughtful and accessible.

How it began

It began with a project at school.

Two years ago, I was asked to create a project on a topic of my choice. I kept coming back to neuroscience, especially autism. I wanted to understand more and share what I learned in a way people would want to watch.

I researched, wrote a script, drew characters and storyboards, then made my first animation. It was the first time I animated a project like this myself. Each drawing helped me find a clearer way to tell the story.

That animation was the beginning. Now I want to create more stories and give other people ways to express their own experiences through art.
